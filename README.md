# Bulgaria Staff Solutions

Create a modern, innovative website for a company that provides trained and insured staff (employed under formal labor contracts, with social security) to partner businesses in Bulgaria — a labor outsourcing / body leasing model. The company also offers an additional service of its own: moving/hauling services ("хамалски услуги").

Target audience: (1) companies looking for reliable staff for their sites/projects, and (2) people looking for work. The site should speak clearly to both, but prioritize the B2B message.

Design requirements:

- Modern, clean, professional style that inspires trust (dark navy/graphite + accent color)

- Hero section with two clear CTA paths: "I need staff" and "I'm looking for work"

- Smooth scroll-triggered animations (fade-in, slide-up)

- "How we work" timeline/steps section with visual progression animation

- Cards showing industry sectors we supply staff to, with hover effects

- A visually distinct section for the moving/hauling services (slightly different accent/style to signal it's a separate service line)

- Animated counters (projects completed, years of experience, partner companies) triggered on scroll

- Short application form for job candidates + separate inquiry form for companies (two clearly separated CTA paths)

- Sticky navigation, smooth scroll to sections

- Floating quick-contact button (Viber/WhatsApp)

- Fully responsive, mobile-first

Content: single-page for now, in Bulgarian language — use placeholder text for now.
