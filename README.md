# Stobot (стобот)

Landing page for a Bulgarian personnel outsourcing and staffing company.

Live at **[stobot16.lovable.app](https://stobot16.lovable.app/)**

## The problem

A staffing company has two customers who want opposite things. Companies want to know you can supply reliable people quickly. Candidates want to know you have work and will treat them properly. Most staffing sites pick one audience and lose the other, or try to serve both and end up vague enough to persuade neither.

## The approach

One page, two clearly separated paths, each with its own language and its own proof. A visitor identifies which one they are within a few seconds and follows that track down the page. No portal, no login, no separate microsite to maintain.

The proof differs by audience. For companies it is scale and speed: workers placed, sites covered, sectors served, and how fast a replacement arrives. For candidates it is the opposite framing, the same operation described as access to work.

**Written in Bulgarian first.** Not translated from English. Staffing copy translated badly reads as foreign in a market where trust is the entire sale.
